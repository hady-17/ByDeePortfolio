Beirut gets strong sun for most of the year. Sun exposure is one of the main causes of uneven tone, dark spots and early lines, and it can undo the work of a good treatment. A daily sunscreen is the simplest way to protect your skin and your results.

## Why it matters around treatments

Many treatments, such as laser, microneedling and peels, leave skin more sensitive to the sun for a while. Sun on freshly treated skin raises the risk of irritation and of dark patches, especially on deeper skin tones. Laser treatments also need skin without a recent tan to be done safely.

## Choosing a sunscreen

- Look for **broad spectrum** protection, which covers both UVA and UVB.
- Choose **SPF 30 or higher** for daily use.
- Pick a texture you like enough to wear every day. The best sunscreen is the one you actually use.
- If your skin is sensitive or has just been treated, ask us which formula suits it.

## Using it properly

- Apply it every morning as the last step of your skin care, even on cloudy days.
- Use enough. For the face, a common guide is about two finger lengths.
- Reapply every two hours when you are outdoors, and after swimming or sweating.
- Add shade, a hat and sunglasses when the sun is strong.

## After a treatment

Follow the aftercare you are given. In general, avoid direct sun on treated skin, wear sunscreen daily and skip tanning until we say your skin is ready.

If you are unsure what your skin needs, we are happy to advise at your next visit.
