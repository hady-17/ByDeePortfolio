Laser hair removal is one of the most popular treatments at ByDee. A little preparation makes each session more comfortable and helps you get the most from your course.

## Before your session

- **Shave the day before.** The laser works on the hair root, so the hair above the skin should be short.
- **Do not wax, pluck or use epilators** for about four weeks before. These remove the root the laser needs to find.
- **Avoid sun and tanning.** Tanned skin raises the risk of side effects, so we may postpone a session if you have a recent tan.
- **Tell us about medication** and any skin conditions, especially medicines that make skin sensitive to light.
- **Come with clean skin**, without creams or deodorant on the area.

## During the session

We check your skin, set the laser for your skin and hair type, and treat the area. Most people describe a warm, snapping feeling. Small areas take about 15 minutes and large areas about an hour.

## Why you need several sessions

Hair grows in cycles, and the laser works best on hair in its growing phase. Not every hair is in that phase at the same time, so sessions are spaced weeks apart to catch each cycle. We confirm your plan at the consultation.

## Afterwards

- Mild redness or warmth for a few hours is common.
- Avoid hot baths, saunas and intense exercise for 24 hours.
- Use gentle, fragrance-free products on the area.
- Protect treated skin from the sun and wear sunscreen.
- Between sessions, shaving is fine. Please avoid waxing and plucking.

If anything feels unusual after a session, contact us.
